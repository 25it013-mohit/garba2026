Place your Navratri MP3 audio files here!

Example:
- dholida.mp3
- sanedo.mp3
- chogada.mp3

Then in `src/data/songsData.ts`, simply set:
src: '/audio/dholida.mp3'

The app automatically supports any standard audio file format (MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC) with full Web Audio frequency visualization and beat detection!
When no MP3 is provided, the app uses its built-in procedural Navratri Garba Dhol & Folk Harmonium Synthesizer!
